## JTBD
When I need a dress for a party this week, I want to know it is actually
available at a nearby Meridian store, so I can plan my trip to collect it
with confidence — without risking a wasted journey.

**User:** click-&-collect shopper (not the store associate).

## Feasibility Gate

| Row | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Client permits AI tools for delivery? | Y |

**Verdict: Conditional** — third-party AI tools (Claude / GPT / Gemini)
permitted only with anonymised inputs; CodeMie pre-approved without restriction.

**Approved tools for this series:** CodeMie Claude, Claude, v0/Lovable.

### Branch 2 — AI in the Product
| Row | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Stock data ready and fresh enough? | Conditional |

**Verdict: Conditional** — stock data is 15–30 min stale — assistant must show
