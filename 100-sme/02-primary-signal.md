## Competitor teardown — Zara click-and-collect flow
**Player:** Zara (Inditex) | **Time:** 5 min | **Date:** 2026-07-27

| Dimension | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Fast local pickup | Solved | Click and Go promises pickup in under 2 hours from selected store |
| Store availability lookup | Partial | Stock shown as tentative — directional, not a hard guarantee |
| Inventory certainty | Unsolved | "Tentative availability" can lead to disappointment or substitution on arrival |
| Cross-store assortment | Unsolved | Shopper constrained to one store's catalog; no cross-store fulfillment |
| Real-time certainty | Unsolved | No live inventory confirmation; promise is speed, not accuracy |

**Gap for the segment:** Even the market benchmark cannot offer real-time inventory
certainty or cross-store assortment access. Mid-market players running fragmented stacks
have less capability than Zara and face the same unsolved customer expectations.

## Pain point re-ratings

**Pain 1 — Compliance cost spike**
Rating: **Sharpened**
The teardown surfaces zero product data, sustainability information, or provenance anywhere
in the click-and-collect flow. The DPP and EPR readiness gap is structural — invisible to
customers today but mandatory-facing as ESPR delegated acts land. The data layer required
for DPP is the same data layer required for unified omnichannel execution.
*Moved by: Zara teardown — no product data layer visible in flow*

**Pain 2 — Store productivity gap**
Rating: **Confirmed**
V2 and V3 directly evidence fulfillment speed failure and post-purchase visibility breakdown.
The teardown confirms the root cause: tentative inventory and no real-time certainty mean
stores cannot reliably fulfil the omnichannel promise even at benchmark scale.
*Moved by: V2 ("delivery takes wayyy tooo long"), V3 (no order tracking after 2 months),
teardown (tentative availability, no real-time confirmation)*

**Pain 3 — Mid-market squeeze**
Rating: **Sharpened**
Zara — the benchmark — leaves real-time inventory certainty and cross-store assortment
unsolved. Mid-market players face identical customer expectations with less infrastructure.
V1 shows that even Zara's browsing UX frustrates shoppers, raising the bar for what
"good enough" looks like in this segment.
*Moved by: V1 (discovery friction on benchmark product), teardown (unsolved gaps
on inventory certainty and cross-store fulfillment)*
