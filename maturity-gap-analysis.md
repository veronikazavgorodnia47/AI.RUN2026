## Gap Analysis

### Gap 1

**Dimension:** Reusability  
**Current level:** L1  
**Why this gap is most damaging:** Without shared design artifacts and prompts, each team member recreates similar AI-assisted work (like user journey maps, personas, accessibility checks), leading to inconsistent outputs and wasted effort across the large, multi-regional project.  
**Root cause:** There is no designated repository for shared AI design artifacts and no established workflow that requires documenting and sharing reusable prompts or templates.

---

### Gap 2

**Dimension:** AI Capabilities  
**Current level:** L1  
**Why this gap is most damaging:** Limited AI integration in design workflows means missing opportunities to efficiently handle the complex regional customization requirements (22 countries with different cultural nuances, payment methods, and user behaviors) that are core to this project's success.  
**Root cause:** No structured approach exists for integrating AI into design deliverables, and AI usage remains ad-hoc rather than being embedded into the design process and team workflows.
