1. **Product.** Meridian Retail Group omnichannel platform — product pages and checkout flow on web and mobile app, backed by SAP inventory and store-level telemetry across 1,400 stores in 22 countries.

2. **Feature.** An AI-powered availability assistant embedded on the product page that estimates real shelf availability at a nearby click-&-collect store before the shopper commits to a reservation.

3. **Target user.** Click-&-collect shoppers who reserve online to avoid a wasted trip to the store — high-intent buyers who have previously arrived at a store to find the reserved item absent, and who distrust the website's displayed stock count as a result.

4. **Expected behaviour.** When a shopper selects "Check in-store availability," the assistant queries the SAP inventory sync and store-level signals, returns a plain-language verdict (Available / Low stock / Uncertain), a visible confidence indicator, and — when stock is uncertain — suggests the nearest store with confirmed availability.

5. **Success signal.** Reduce phantom-stock cancellations at pickup from ~7% to ≤2% of click-&-collect orders within 90 days of launch; tracked in the order-management system as cancellation reason "item not on shelf at collection."

6. **Out of scope.** Real-time reserve-and-hold (slot locking), in-store staff notifications, stock replenishment triggers, and availability checks for delivery (non-click-&-collect) orders.
