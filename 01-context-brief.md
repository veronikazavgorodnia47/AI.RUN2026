## Playground context
1. Mid-market EU omnichannel fashion retail
2. Western Europe
3. €1–5B revenue
4. GDPR and ESPR compliance pressure across 22-country stack
5. Fulfillment cost per order 40% above EU fashion sector median due to fragmented country stacks

---

## Market trends
- Traceability and transparency moved to the center of strategy — product data and provenance
  are now strategic, not just compliance topics. (Strategy&, 2026)
- Store networks are becoming smaller and more productive; physical footprint must justify
  itself through omnichannel fulfillment and brand experience, not traffic alone.
- Market is splitting between premium-upmarket and value-led demand, compressing the
  undifferentiated mid-market.

## Competitive moves
- Inditex FY2025: online sales rose 4.8% to €10.7B while opening stores in 41 markets —
  a balanced stores-plus-digital model that sets the benchmark mid-market players cannot
  replicate without unified inventory. (Inditex FY2025 results, 2026-03-07)

## Regulatory shifts
- EU Waste Framework Directive approved 9 Sep 2025, textile EPR in force 16 Oct 2025 —
  mandatory extended producer responsibility for textiles and footwear across the EU.
- ESPR Digital Product Passport: textiles in first-wave priority group; product-specific
  DPP rules still in delegated acts phase.
- EU Empowering Consumers Directive applies from 27 Sep 2026; separate Green Claims
  Directive proposal withdrawn Jun 2025.

---

## Strategic pain points

**1. Compliance cost spike**
EPR and DPP require SKU-level traceability data and end-of-life reporting. A retailer
running 22 siloed country stacks cannot generate this data without a unified product layer.
*Source: EU Waste Framework Directive, Sep 2025*

**2. Store productivity gap**
Stores need to earn their cost through click-and-collect, returns, and fulfillment — but
fragmented inventory visibility makes omnichannel execution impossible at the unit level.
Inditex's stores-plus-digital model shows the benchmark; mid-market players running
fragmented stacks cannot replicate it.
*Source: Inditex FY2025, Mar 2026*

**3. Mid-market squeeze**
Value-led players (Shein, Temu) attack the price floor; Zalando moves upmarket. Strategy&
flags "smart value" and premium polarization as the dominant 2026 dynamic. Undifferentiated
€1–5B retailers lose margin from both ends without differentiated positioning.
*Source: Strategy& 2026 Fashion Retail Trends*
